# Sources

## Code lists in `model/vocabularies.json`, also used in the `dspace/` forms

Retrieved on 2026-10-30. Each section of the file names its exact source.

| Section | Source | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| `regulation1089`, `themes`, `limitations`, `topicCategories` | INSPIRE registry (https://inspire.ec.europa.eu/registry) and INSPIRE schema enumerations | European Commission; reuse under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU |
| `ford` | OECD Fields of Research and Development, as published for CCMM at https://github.com/techlib/NKR-vocabs | the repository states no licence |
| `fileTypes`, `languages.eu` | EU Vocabularies, Publications Office of the European Union: tables "File type" and "Language" | reuse under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU |
| `languages.iso6392` | ISO 639-2 code list of the Library of Congress, the ISO 639-2 Registration Authority (https://www.loc.gov/standards/iso639-2/) | published for free use |

The access rights in `model/geoccmm-core.json` are the COAR Access Rights vocabulary (https://vocabularies.coar-repositories.org/access_rights/), © COAR, CC BY 4.0.

Some form labels are GeoCCMM's own wording, not official translations: both languages for the INSPIRE limitation grounds, the access categories and the relations, and the Czech labels of service types.
